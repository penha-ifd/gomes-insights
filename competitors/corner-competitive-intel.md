## Product

- **UGC-only social map** — places only appear on map when a user saves/recommends them
- Reviews with text, photos, and curated lists
- AI-powered personalized search
- Booking integration (provider TBD)
- Peer-to-peer recommendations, not algorithmic feed
- No leaderboards, no streaks, no gamification
- No text-based search — purely map-discovery

## Competitive Positioning

- **Pitch:** "Social alternative to Google Maps for Gen Z"
- **Target:** 18-25 year olds discovering IRL meetup spots
- **Differentiator vs Google Maps:** social proof from friends, not SEO'd business listings
- **Differentiator vs Yelp:** visual, map-first, no star ratings
- **Differentiator vs Beli:** map-centric vs ranking-centric; no gamification
